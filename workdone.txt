    - Pupil: physiological bounds [1.5mm, 8.5mm], computed mean, std, skew, kurtosis
    - Gaze: spatial entropy across cockpit instrument scanning axes

  * delta_HR, delta_HRV_SDNN, delta_HRV_RMSSD, delta_HRV_LF_HF
  * delta_EDA_tonic, delta_EDA_conductance
  * delta_EMG_flexor, delta_EMG_extensor

- Purpose: Removes inter-subject physiological offsets without data leakage.
  A pilot with resting HR=55bpm vs another with HR=80bpm will both show the same
  delta_HR response to workload, making the model generalizable across individuals.

    Oculomotor_Only GBDT (40.66%) OUTPERFORMS Multimodal (40.15%) and Autonomic_Only (32.07%).
    Adding ECG/EDA to eye features does NOT help and can slightly hurt.

    Personalized_Delta_Only: 31.06%  vs  Raw_Autonomic_Only: 29.17%
    Pre-subtracting resting baselines gives only marginal gain (+1.9%) for autonomic features.
    Multivariate models learn subject offsets from raw features nearly as well.
    Paper line: "Univariate baseline normalization did not resolve inter-subject ECG/EDA

  FINDING 3 - Parsimonious 8-Feature Model is Nearly as Good as 28-Feature Full Model:
    Parsimonious (8 feats) RF: 38.38%  vs  Multimodal (28 feats) RF: 38.89%
    Only -0.51% accuracy loss. The compact equation retains 99% of predictive value.
    This strongly supports using the parsimonious model for the interpretable paper equation.

  FINDING 4 - Tunnel Vision Pattern Under Extreme Workload:
    Under Level 4 (severe), pilots INCREASE fixation frequency (fix_density_mean OR=2.33)
    but DECREASE scan variability (fix_density_stdev OR=0.42).
    Confirms aviation cognitive psychology: high load -> fixation -> tunnel vision.
    This is a novel, neurophysiologically meaningful result.

Script: advanced_workload_modeling.py
Evaluated on: 34 clean pilot folds (sub-cp003 excluded from eye models)

--- Methodological Enhancements Implemented ---
  1. Bilateral Eye Feature Fusion:
     Averaged Left + Right eye coordinates (gaze_entropy_X/Y/Z, psd_max_X/Y/Z, pupil stats, blink rate)
     to denoise bilateral sensor noise without discarding either eye channel.
  2. Targeted Modality Fusion (Oculomotor + Forearm EMG):
     Blends visual cognitive load (LC-NE pupil & gaze dynamics) with physical stick grip strain
     (emg_flexor_rms, emg_extensor_rms), completely omitting plateaued ECG/EDA signals.
  3. Multi-Faceted Metric Suite:
     - Strict 4-Class Accuracy & Macro F1 (exact classification)
     - Adjacent Accuracy (|y_true - y_pred| <= 1): Standard ordinal metric for +/- 1 difficulty tolerance
     - Binary Low vs. High Workload (Levels 1-2 vs. Levels 3-4)
     - Extreme Binary Discrimination (Level 1 vs. Level 4)
     - Multi-Class One-vs-Rest Macro ROC-AUC
  4. Dual Granularity Analysis:
     - Pipeline 1: Run-Level Dataset (394 noisy runs)
     - Pipeline 2: Denoised Subject x Difficulty Aggregated Dataset (136 clean mean samples)
  5. Soft-Voting Ensemble:
     Weighted probabilistic combination of GBDT (40%) + RF (35%) + Calibrated SVM (25%).
